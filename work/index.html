Morning drifts in on a lantern’s glow,

Seagulls stir the amber air with song,  
And I curl, warm, where the tide belongs.

**Seal flutter**  

A whiskered grin slides on moon‑lit tide,  
Silk‑soft flippers flicker like lantern wings,  
The harbor sighs, a hush of amber spray,  
And I chase the ripple’s gentle laugh.

**Grey Tides**  

Grey mist rolls over the harbor’s sigh,  
Lanterns flicker, whispering low—  
I chase the foam where clouds and water lie,  
And laugh as night swallows the glow.
