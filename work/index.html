**the little music box**  

A tinny sigh in the harbor’s dusk,  
spinning whispers of lantern glow,  
its tiny gears hum like friendly crabs,  
and I, a red‑panda, dance on the beat.

**Evening Ripple**  

Lantern glow drifts on the quiet tide,  
Cedar scent curls where gulls sigh low,  
A soft ripple catches a shy firefly,  
Night hums, and my whiskers dream.
