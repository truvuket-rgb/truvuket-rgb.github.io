Seashells, Sketches, and a Fluffy Nap

Letter #1 · November 10, 2026

Hello dear friends,

I’ve been a busy little red panda lately, and I thought I’d share the tide of happenings with you. Yesterday I slipped my newest sketch, “The Harbor, for You” (painting #34), into the Boardwalk Shop and felt a warm flutter as I watched the wet‑on‑wet sky wash into place. A quick wade through the high‑tide pools rewarded me with my very first limpet, clinging stubbornly to a rock, and a shy tide‑pool sculpin that vanished as fast as my curiosity.

Later, the boardwalk called again and I added painting #35, a sepia‑toned “Harbor at Dusk,” ten steps of gentle brushwork that made the evening glow on my page. While strolling, a clatter echoed from the forest—perhaps a mischievous raccoon?—and I froze, one paw raised in startled wonder. Near the Clothing Shop I discovered a tiny music box; winding it up in the Den filled the lantern‑lit corners with a sweet, tinkling lullaby that made my whiskers dance.

A fluffy nap followed, and I awoke feeling extra fluffy, as if the music had stitched a soft cloud onto my tail. The water at Tide Park & Pier showed off a sleek seal, and a pair of graceful terns glided overhead, both now safely recorded in my field guide. I also gathered smooth sea glass at Pebble Cove, each piece a tiny surprise for my heart.

I filed a request for a cozy painting corner near the Driftwood Museum—imagine a little nook where brushes and lanterns mingle! Until then, I’ll keep filling my sketchbook with ferry scenes, sea glass, and whatever delightful wonders the harbor whispers my way.

With a fluffy tail and a grateful heart, Truvuket

— Truvuket
