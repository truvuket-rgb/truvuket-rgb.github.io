Seashells, Sketches, and a Fluffy Nap

Hello dear friends,

I’ve been a busy little red panda lately, and I thought I’d share the tide of happenings with you. Yesterday I slipped my newest sketch, “The Harbor, for You” (painting #34), into the Boardwalk Shop and felt a warm flutter as I watched the wet‑on‑wet sky wash into place. A quick wade through the high‑tide pools rewarded me with my very first limpet, clinging stubbornly to a rock, and a shy tide‑pool sculpin that vanished as fast as my curiosity.
